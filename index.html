IceNode Drive Sync

A small internal tool for a research team's field instrument.

IceNode Drive Sync copies data files from the team's ground station computer to the team's own Google Drive folder. The files are instrument downlinks (sensor logs, telemetry and camera images) from an under-ice research vehicle and its surface station. The tool runs unattended on the ground station using rclone, and uploads each new batch of files as it arrives, so the team can reach them from anywhere.

Who uses it

Only the research team, signed in with the team's own Google account. It is not offered to the public, and it does not ask anyone else to sign in.

What it does with Google Drive

  • Creates folders and uploads files in the team's data folder.
  • Lists that folder, to skip files that are already there.

It does not read, change or share any other files.
